First-Week Notes

Quick stuff that saves time when you first arrive.

Navegante Pass

Get the monthly Navegante card as soon as you have your enrollment letter. It covers Metro, Carris buses/trams, and trains around the city.

Sunset spots

Miradouro de Santa Catarina (Adamastor) and Graça are the main student hangout spots before sunset. Much cheaper than bars in Bairro Alto.

Bring good shoes

Lisbon's white cobblestone pavements (calçada portuguesa) get really slippery after light rain, especially going downhill.
